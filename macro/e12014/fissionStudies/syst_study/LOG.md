## 2026-10-20

- **Scope (user).** Step 1 of the plan's order of work only: P3, P5 with C4, P6, P7, then the baseline (±10° sample with the reference fit, the recommended fit and the recommended fit with another seed; the 10–30° sample). Stop when the baseline is fitted, reported, committed and pushed.
- **Comparisons are statistical (user).** Same seeds keep events in step only until a variation changes a random draw, and almost every change does (for example, the simulation redraws an event when a fragment ends within 50 mm of the axis, and a variation can change which events pass). That is fine: every event has a known true Z, angle and depth and counts in its cell. No code to keep events in step or to match them one to one.
- **C4 on a sample of its own (user: smaller separate sample; size my choice).** 1000 events (20 chunks) with the baseline's Z and angle ranges, simulated at 939.0 and 931.5 MeV per mass unit with the same seeds, both fitted at the recommended settings (`studies/syst_c4.ini`).
- **CATIMA (user).** `AtELossCATIMA` (a model class, not a table) is to be the third energy-loss model in B1, next to LISE and SRIM. B1 is step 2; nothing done for it now.
- **P6 done.** The local `tpcSharedInfo/eLoss/LISE/83_200.txt` was the ¹⁴²Pr table; replaced with the cluster's ²⁰⁰Bi table (same checksum now). The other shared inputs agree with the cluster's: every LISE and SRIM table has the same checksum, `respAvg.root` too, and the zap list differs only in line endings. The local copy has one extra file, `SRIM/83_200.txt`, which no study uses (the beam uses LISE).
- **P3: `sim.decayAngleMin`, `sim.decayAngleMax`.** Each event draws θ_CM uniformly between them; `sim.decayAngle` keeps its meaning when no range is set. The 10–30° sample is two simulations, 60–80° and 100–120°, 750 events each, uniform in θ (as the ±10° sample).
- **P5: `sim.massUnit`.** Default: the fit's own value, `AtTools::Kinematics::AtoE(1)` = 931.5 MeV, so the simulation and the fit share one number. 939.0 gives the old behavior.
- **C4 prediction, before any result: no measurable effect.** A local check (40 events, same seed) gives the same lab fragment energies at both mass units (1141.86 MeV, 9.67681 against 9.67678 MeV per nucleon). Without relativity, the beam velocity (fixed kinetic energy at the window) and the fragments' center-of-mass velocities (fixed TKE) all scale as 1/√(mass unit), so every lab kinetic energy ½mv² is independent of it; the energy-loss tables are indexed by kinetic energy, so the tracks are unchanged apart from relativistic terms of order 10⁻⁴. The plan's statement that the masses give "the wrong velocities for a given kinetic energy" holds, but no part of the simulation uses a velocity at fixed energy.
- **P7: `sim.elossScale`** multiplies every fragment's dE/dx, not the beam's, through `AtELossTable::SetDensity`. The LISE tables are now loaded with a nominal density of 1 instead of 0 (same units, MeV/µm), so `SetDensity` can scale them. LISE only: SRIM tables keep their density private, and B2 uses LISE. Checked: dE/dx × 1.05000 and range × 0.95238 at 50, 200 and 500 MeV.
- **Library fix for P7.** `AtELossTable::SetDensity` crashed on any table without range variance (every LISE table): it rebuilt the range-variance spline from an empty vector. It now skips that spline when there is none, and logs the old density instead of the new one twice. New unit test `SetDensityWithoutRangeVariance`; all 35 AtTools tests pass.
- **`dump_fit.C` writes the true vertex depth** (`vz`, mm from the window: SimInfo's `vZ`, uniform over about 0–690 mm with `zCutoff` = 300), and gives events without a fit their true angle and depth.
- **Pseudo-data detail as in the tuning study:** 0.25 mm steps and 4000 diffusion points per line (the plan's 6 CPU-s per simulated event assumes this).
- **Seeds.** Explicit for every section, 10⁴ apart, so the chunk seeds (seed + k) never overlap: `syst_base.ini` sim 510000, digi 520000, fit 530000 (`rec2` 540000), the 10–30° samples 550000–580000; `syst_c4.ini` 610000–630000. Every fit of the baseline uses the recommended settings except `ref`.
- **Local smoke test.** 4 events through simulation (with `sim.elossScale = 1.02`), digitization, fit and `dump_fit.C`: runs, and the scale is applied.
