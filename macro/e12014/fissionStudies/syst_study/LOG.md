Without relativity, the beam velocity (fixed kinetic energy at the window) and the fragments' center-of-mass velocities (fixed TKE) all scale as 1/√(mass unit), so every lab kinetic energy ½mv² is independent of it; the energy-loss tables are indexed by kinetic energy, so the tracks are unchanged apart from relativistic terms of order 10⁻⁴. The plan's statement that the masses give "the wrong velocities for a given kinetic energy" holds, but no part of the simulation uses a velocity at fixed energy.

Checked: dE/dx × 1.05000 and range × 0.95238 at 50, 200 and 500 MeV.
- **Library fix for P7.** `AtELossTable::SetDensity` crashed on any table without range variance (every LISE table): it rebuilt the range-variance spline from an empty vector.
